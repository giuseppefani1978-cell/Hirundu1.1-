all tutorial steps and practice dialogs at 200%, camera/QR view at 200%, or a
full ten-level run. No claim of full A06 acceptance.

   scroll to the last controls, then return to normal scale. Repeat landscape.
2. Visit Settings: installation instructions should be in the page, without a
   popup. Launch from the Home Screen: that section should be absent.
3. At enlarged text sizes, check each guide step, cards, card selection and QR;
   verify every action is reachable. Check French, Italian and English labels
   (Spanish is preserved too).
4. Launch a classic, rebound and flight level; check pads, framing, rotation and
   the existing top Pause button. No tap-to-pause is added.
5. Confirm the original full intro starts normally and still has its fallback
   in Low Power Mode. A04 is excluded, so no cinematic shortening is expected.
